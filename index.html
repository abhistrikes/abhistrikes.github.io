Projects

Robust Airport Gate Assignment under Delay Uncertainty

Oct'26 – Present · Python, Gurobi, scikit-learn

  • Built an estimate-then-optimise pipeline: gradient-boosting quantile models of arrival/departure delay (trained on 23,900 flights) feed a stochastic MILP (Şeker & Noyan, 2012) solved in Gurobi.
  • Generated 1,000 delay scenarios by residual bootstrap and reduced them to 30 with k-means. A scenario-aggregation reformulation keeps model size independent of the number of scenarios.
  • A rolling-horizon heuristic warm-starts the exact MILP, cutting solve time about 4×.
  • Over 500 out-of-sample days, cut expected gate conflicts by 49% and overlap minutes by 36% vs. the schedule-based plan.

Gate Assignment IP – Chong & Nordin (2015) Replication

Aug'26 – Sep'26 · IBM CPLEX (OPL, DOcplex)

  • Formulated the integer program in OPL and Python DOcplex and reproduced all 3 published optimal solutions after finding and correcting 2 data errors in the paper.
  • Proved total unimodularity of the constraint matrix and mapped 8 distinct optimal assignments across the full range of objective weights.

Bearing Predictive Maintenance with an LLM Assistant

Oct'26 · Python, scikit-learn, LLM

  • Turned the NASA IMS run-to-failure vibration data (3,140 recordings, 20 kHz) into time-domain and envelope-spectrum features.
  • An Isolation Forest trained only on healthy data flagged all 3 bearing failures 55–173 hours in advance with no false alarms (leave-one-bearing-out validation).
  • An LLM turns the model's evidence report into plain-language maintenance advice for technicians.

Customer Subscription Prediction – Replication Study

Oct'26 · R

  • Replicated 5 classifiers from a published study and exposed target leakage: without the leaked features, accuracy fell from 0.84 to the 0.73 majority baseline and ROC-AUC from 0.90 to about 0.50.
